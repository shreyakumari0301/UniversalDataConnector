Voice Chat

Select your company, then click Speak. Your speech is sent to POST /chat; the assistant uses your company’s data and the reply is spoken aloud (TTS).

Must match where your API is running (use http://localhost:8000 if started with uvicorn or start_demo.sh).

Data returned is scoped to this company (sent as X-Company-ID).

If you set API_KEYS_JSON, put your key here (sent as X-API-Key).
